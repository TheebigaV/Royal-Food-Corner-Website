Special Menu

"Explore our menu, where every dish tells a story of flavor, creativity, and culinary passion. Your journey through taste begins here."

Dosa

A South Indian delicacy, dosa is a thin, crispy pancake made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It's often served with flavorful chutneys and sambar.

Idli

Idli is a soft and spongy steamed rice cake, also originating from South India. These white, fluffy cakes are typically enjoyed with sambar and coconut chutney.

Vada

Vada is a popular Indian snack made by deep-frying a spiced and savory lentil or gram flour batter. It's crispy on the outside and soft on the inside.

Pani puri

Pani Puri is a beloved Indian street food consisting of hollow, crispy puris filled with a spicy and tangy tamarind water, along with potatoes and chickpeas.

Noodles

Noodles, originating from Chinese cuisine, come in various forms and flavors. They are typically stir-fried or served in soups, making for a versatile and delicious dish.

Soup

Soups are warm, comforting, and highly customizable. They come in countless flavors and varieties, from creamy bisques to clear broths, offering a soothing start or complement to a meal.

String Hopper

String hoppers are a Sri Lankan specialty, made from rice flour dough that's squeezed into thin, delicate noodles. They are often served with curries or coconut sambol.

Kothu Roti

A popular Sri Lankan street food, kothu roti features chopped and stir-fried flatbread mixed with an assortment of spices, vegetables, and sometimes meat, creating a flavorful medley.

Bun

Buns can vary widely in flavor and use. They can be sweet or savory, and are used for sandwiches, burgers, or as a side with dishes like pulled pork.
