Our History

Where The Food’s As Good As The Root Beer.

Satisfying people hunger for simple pleasures

Our restaurant, established in 1965, started as a family-owned diner known for comfort food. Over the decades, it transformed into a cozy Italian trattoria and has since become a modern dining destination, celebrated for its global fusion and dedication to culinary excellence. While evolving, it has always been a beloved gathering spot for friends and family, where good food and great company thrive. Our enduring commitment to quality, creativity, and warm hospitality is a testament to our heritage, making our restaurant a place to create lasting memories.
